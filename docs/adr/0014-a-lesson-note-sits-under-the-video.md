# A Lesson's Note sits under the video

The Student's Note on a Lesson sits directly under the video, above the Lesson's written content, so the Student can write while they watch without scrolling away from the player. It is styled like the Questions & Discussion card at the bottom of the page (the same header, padding, character counter and helper line), so the two writing surfaces read as one design, but it stays where it is.

## Considered Options

- **Move the Note down beside Questions & Discussion**: rejected, because the Student would have to scroll past the content, quiz and completion button to write while the video plays. Feedback asked for the Note to be "more integrated with the bit at the bottom"; matching that card's look answers it without losing the position.
